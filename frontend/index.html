          How MODIS and VIIRS were combined

          About helios

          The challenge

          NASA Space Apps 2026: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots.

          MODIS has tracked fires from space since 2000, but it is getting old. VIIRS is taking its place. The two count fires differently, so you can't just put their data together. We made one fire record out of both, so you can see how fire changes over the years.

          What helios does

          • Combines MODIS and VIIRS fire data into one record, 2000 to today
          • Shows every month as a calendar, so you can see the busy months
          • Tells you if this month is busier than past years
          • Lets you type a place or draw your own area
          • Downloads the data as CSV files and images
          • Has a color-blind safe color option
          • 3D models of both satellites, with their parts explained
